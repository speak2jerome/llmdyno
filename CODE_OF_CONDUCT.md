# Code of conduct

LLMDyno follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people, and keep discussions on topic.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report problems privately to speak2jerome@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may warn,
remove content from, or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
